## Section 2 Copyright

(1) The copyright in the Software remains with the Developer. Under section 29(1) UrhG, copyright itself cannot be transferred between living persons; this agreement grants usage rights only (section 29(2), section 31 UrhG).

(2) The Developer has the right to be recognized as author (section 13 UrhG). The parties regulate the author credit in Section 6.

## Section 3 Grant of usage rights

(1) The Developer grants LineFM a [non-exclusive | exclusive] usage right in the Software (section 31(1) to (3) UrhG), territorially [worldwide] and temporally [unlimited | until …], for the following types of use (section 69c UrhG):

1. reproduction, including loading, displaying, running and storing (section 69c no. 1 UrhG);
2. adaptation, translation and other alteration, and use of the results (section 69c no. 2 UrhG) [restrict if applicable];
3. distribution, in particular through app stores (Google Play, Apple App Store, Microsoft Store, Snapcraft, Flathub) and as a download (section 69c no. 3 UrhG);
4. making available to the public, in particular as a web app (section 69c no. 4 UrhG).

(2) Because under section 31(5) UrhG the scope is, in case of doubt, determined by the purpose of the contract, the types of use above are listed exhaustively. The purpose of the contract is the operation and publication of the app for LineFM's radio offering.

(3) Rights to unknown types of use (section 31a UrhG) are [not granted | granted; in that case observe the written form and the right of revocation under section 31a(1) UrhG].

(2) In the event of a transfer, the duty to credit the author under Section 6 remains in place; LineFM obliges the acquirer accordingly. [Have liability under section 34(4) UrhG reviewed by a lawyer.]

## Notes for the legal review

- Non-exclusive or exclusive usage right (section 31(2) and (3) UrhG) and the relationship to the proprietary `LICENSE` and to the publicly visible source code.
- Scope of the adaptation rights (section 69c no. 2 UrhG): may LineFM develop the app further without the Developer?
- Consent to onward transfer and sublicenses (sections 34, 35 UrhG), liability on transfer (section 34(4) UrhG).
- Remuneration with regard to sections 32, 32a UrhG; clarify the tax treatment with the tax adviser.
- Validity and enforceability of the author credit (section 13 UrhG) and of the credit clause in `NOTICE`.
- Whether the Developer acted as an employee or on commission (section 69b UrhG assigns the rights to the employer for employees) and whether co-authors are involved (section 8 UrhG).
