About this app

LINEFM Radio plays the programmes of the web radio LINEFM and of the partner stations Radio3FM and FobFM. Radio3FM and FobFM are stations of other operators, not LINEFM stations. LineFM is an offering of RadioGruppe Deutschland on behalf of the LINEFM Group. LineFM Studios is owned by Julian Gebauer.

radiogruppe.de

Tim Hauke (VectoDE) developed the app for LineFM Studios.

Music and licence

The programmes run through the platform laut.fm. Licensing of the music played lies with the respective stations or with laut.fm. The app plays the laut.fm stream and shows the track information provided there. It does not store or copy any music.

Rights to names, logos and covers

Station names and logos belong to their respective operators. Album covers come from Apple (iTunes Search API); the rights to them rest with their respective owners.

Consumer dispute resolution

We do not take part in dispute resolution proceedings before a consumer arbitration board and are not obliged to.
